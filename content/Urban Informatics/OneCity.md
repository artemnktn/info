OneCity is a webGIS that allows developers and businesses to identify, evaluate and compare investment opportunities that align with official spatial strategies. OneCity brings together information sources used by developers and urban planners on a single platform in an interactive cartographic interface with all the information the developer needs abot a land plot. 

In the NOVAYAlabs team, I'm working as a project manager, conceptualising a digital tool that promotes transparent and data-driven urban transformation.
