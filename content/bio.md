### not too short bio (150 words):
Artem Nikitin is an urban researcher and architect based in Hong Kong, where he is pursuing a PhD at the [School of Design, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sd/). His work links spatial data science and critical urban theory, using GIS, web cartography, and spatial modelling to study how urban policy impacts land and property values, urban form, and models of governance. 

He trained as an architect at the St. Petersburg State University of Architecture and Civil Engineering and later completed an MA in Smart Cities and Urban Informatics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, where he was a Research Fellow at the [Algorithmic City Planning Lab](https://algocitylab.com/). He also holds a postgraduate diploma in Speculative Urbanism from the [Strelka Institute](https://www.e-flux.com/directory/69632/strelka-institute-for-media-architecture-and-design) in Moscow. Before his doctoral studies, he spent over seven years as a lead city planner and strategist at [Novaya Labs](https://novaya.co.uk/), where he led urban projects across Russia, South Africa, and the UK.
