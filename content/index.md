I am an urban researcher and architect based in Hong Kong, where I am pursuing my PhD at the [School of Design](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sd/?sc_lang=en), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. 

Merging spatial data science with critical urban theory, I work with GIS, web cartography, and spatial modelling to examine how urban form and planning instruments actively reshape conventional design practice and governance. I previously led multidisciplinary planning and policy projects at[ Novaya labs ](https://novaya.co.uk/)and[ OneCity](https://onecity.dev/), and co-hosted the [впесочнице](https://sandbox.mave.digital/) podcast on speculative urbanism.
