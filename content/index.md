I am an urban researcher and designer, with background in architecture. 

Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU's School of Design. Before, I worked as a lead urban planner with Novaya Labs, and as a research fellow at the ACP Lab (Geography, HUJI). 

Working across critical urban theories and spatial data science, my research focuses on housing, property, and urban morphology in Hong Kong’s high-density, vertical context. Alongside that, I'm drawn to counter-cartography and other mapping practices, using GIS, spatial modelling and data visualisation.

This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes, code snippets and occasionally skating videos.
