I am an urban researcher and designer with a background in architecture.

Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU's School of Design. Before, I worked as a lead urban planner with Novaya Labs, and as a research fellow at the ACP Lab (Geography, HUJI). 

Working across (critical) urban theories and spatial data science, my research focuses on housing and urban morphology in Hong Kong’s high-density, vertical context. I use a design-research methodology, drawing on interdisciplinary perspectives in spatial political economy, speculative cartography, urban informatics and geospatial technologies.

This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes, maps and visualisations.
