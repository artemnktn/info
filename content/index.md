I am an urban researcher and designer, with background in architecture. Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU's School of Design. Before this, I worked as a lead urban planner with Novaya Labs, and as a research fellow at the ACP Lab (Geography, HUJI). 

Working across critical urban theories and spatial data science, my research focuses on housing, property rights, and urban morphology in Hong Kong’s high-density, vertical context. I'm also drawn to experimental cartography and mapping, using GIS, spatial modelling, and emerging visualisation techniques.

This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes, and maps.
