I am an urban researcher and designer with main focus in urban informatics, spatial justice and the political economy of urban form.

Using spatial data science and critical design methods, I analyse how urban governance is mediated through data, spatial models, and planning instruments. Alongside my architectural training, I hold an MA in Smart Cities and Urban Informatics and have over seven years of experience working on city planning and web-GIS projects across different geographies.

Currently, I am based in Hong Kong, where I am pursuing a PhD at the School of Design, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
