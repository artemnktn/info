I am an urban researcher and designer with research interests in urban informatics and critical theory. Using spatial data science and design methods, I examine housing affordability and questions of justice in urban development. Alongside my architectural training, I hold an MA in Smart Cities and Urban Informatics and have over seven years of experience working on urban planning and web-GIS projects across different geographies.

Currently, I am based in Hong Kong, where I am pursuing a PhD at the School of Design, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
