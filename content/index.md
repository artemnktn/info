I am an urban researcher and architect based in Hong Kong, where I am pursuing my PhD at the [School of Design](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sd/?sc_lang=en), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. 

Linking spatial data science and critical urban theory in design and planning, I use GIS, web cartography, and spatial modelling to analyse how urban policy and emerging technologies impact land and property values, built form, and models of governance. I previously led multidisciplinary city planning and policy projects at[ Novaya labs ](https://novaya.co.uk/)and worked as an urban strategist at [ OneCity](https://onecity.dev/).
