 I am an urban researcher and designer with a background in architecture.  Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU’s School of Design. Before PolyU, I worked for 7 years as an urban planner leading multidisciplinary design and urban policy projects grounded in spatial analysis.  
  
Working across (critical) urban theories and data science, my research today focuses on spatial political economy of urban form in Hong Kong’s high-density, volumetric context. I use a design-research methodology, drawing on interdisciplinary perspectives in speculative cartography and mapping, using GIS, spatial modelling, and emerging visualisation techniques.  
  
This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I publish news and collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes and visualisations.
