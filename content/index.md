Alongside my architectural training, I hold an *MA in Smart Cities and Urban Informatics* from HUJI and have over seven years of experience working on city planning and web-GIS projects across different geographies.

Currently, I am based in Hong Kong, where I am pursuing a PhD at the *School of Design, HKPU.*
