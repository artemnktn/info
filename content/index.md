 I am an urban researcher and designer with a background in architecture. Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU’s School of Design, linking (critical) urban theories with spatial data science and emerging visualisation techniques. Before that, I worked for seven years as an urban planner, leading multidisciplinary design and urban policy projects grounded in spatial analysis.  
  
This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I publish news and collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes, maps and visualisations.
