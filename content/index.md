I'm a spatial researcher and designer with a background in architecture. Currently based in Hong Kong, doing a PhD at PolyU’s School of Design, linking (critical) urban theories with spatial data science and emerging geo/visualisation techniques. Before that, I worked for seven years as an urban planner, leading multidisciplinary design and policy projects grounded in spatial analysis.  
  
This is my digital garden (mostly sharing some of my Obsidian notes) where I publish news and collect ongoing and past projects, writings, notes, maps and visualisations. Most pages are still hidden, as I'm slowly working on updating the site, but everything will be up soon!
